ArborForm explores the intersection of structural engineering, digital fabrication, and sculptural design. By combining multiple materials into a unified system, it demonstrates how structural elements can become expressive architectural forms.

This project investigates new fabrication techniques and the potential for hybrid construction methods that merge aesthetics with structural performance.

The Project

ArborForm is designed as a multi-material structural sculpture, integrating engineered components with organic geometries. The project aims to challenge conventional perceptions of structural systems by treating them as both functional and artistic elements.
