Who are we?

We are students from the University of Southampton. Our goal is to create a structure inspired by the ASCI structural sculpture to encompass all engineering disciplines on Boldrewood Campus

Our Product

ArborForm at its core is an educational piece, bridging the gap between nature and construction, a visionary project that redefines the way we interact with our built environment.

At its core, ArborForm is a living architectural marvel, seamlessly integrating lush greenery with innovative design. This project is a testament to our commitment to sustainability, as it not only enhances the aesthetic appeal of urban spaces but also promotes environmental well-being.
